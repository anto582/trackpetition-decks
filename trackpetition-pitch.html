The Solution

One platform that watches every case.

TrackPetition reads case status straight from the official USCIS API and turns manual tracking into automated intelligence.

Official USCIS API Integration

We read the same official status USCIS publishes, checked automatically on a fixed schedule. USCIS typically posts a change online before the paper notice is mailed — when it does, you see it that day instead of when the envelope arrives.

Client Communications

Every alert reaches the people you nominate, in English or Spanish, carrying the new status, the exact time USCIS posted it, and what it means in plain language. In design Putting your firm's logo, colors and contact on those alerts — and a white-label client portal on its own subdomain — are both still being designed. Neither is part of what ships today.

Dual-Speed Tracking

Daily verification for standard cases — hourly checks for urgent or expedited petitions.

Omnichannel Alerts

Sent as soon as we see the change, over Email, Telegram & WhatsApp — with more channels arriving in the next release.

Case Timeline with Intervals

Every case shows its full status history with the exact gap between each stage — 4d, 15mo, 3mo — so a paralegal sees at a glance how long a case has been sitting where it is.

Managed Case Control

Case tiering & intelligent routing.

Set the rules for every receipt and every alert — we run them for you.

Per-Case Tier Control

Flip any receipt from Standard to Premium on request — ideal for I-140 Premium Processing or active RFE timelines.

Full Caseload, Managed

Send your caseload as a CSV — we import, classify, and keep it current for you.

Configurable Recipients

In design Splitting one case's alerts between the lead attorney, assigned paralegal, petitioner and beneficiary is being designed. Today every alert goes to the addresses and numbers held on the account.

Monthly Audit Report — PDF

Every check timestamped — availability, logged incidents, an activity map, and the exact date and hour USCIS changed each status. Delivered monthly as a PDF you hand the client as proof the case is watched.
